# Signal plays

Four rules. Fit first, then a reason to reach out now. Sort that reason into one of three kinds. Write about their situation. Keep the trigger to yourself.

## The four rules

1. Fit first. `column.ai_fit_score` before any spend on a reason. Unknown fit is a hold.
2. Three kinds of reason. Standing fit (they match the Ideal Customer and nothing new happened). A recent change at the company (a job post from `source.linkedin_jobs` at 1 credit a row, or a research answer). Direct interest in you (`source.linkedin_profile_viewers` and `source.linkedin_followers` are 0 on the card where a price is stated, `source.engaged_with_account` is 1 a row, `source.watched_company_page_posts` is 0.4 a row).
3. Write about their situation. The trigger stays in the worksheet. The note does not announce it. Tone comes from Company Context.
4. One message shape. An observation, the problem in their words, what changes, one small ask.

Funding and tech-stack change are not a source on the card today. Use `column.research` or `column.web_search` (4 credits a row on the web search card) and quote it. `column.research` says the price varies. `integrations.signal_sources.list` is the free list of connected signal sources. `crm.signals.search_run` is dynamic. dry_run first, quote, wait for the owner's yes on that item.

## What needs a yes

Any live paid source or column. A note is still unsent.
