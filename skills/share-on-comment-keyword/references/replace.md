# Replace

## Inputs

expected_revision from the list call, platform, artifact_id, and the full rules array.

## Call

social.outreach.keyword_dm_rules.replace is 0 credits and destructive. dry_run first, quote, wait for the owner's yes on that item. A stale revision is refused. Re-read. Do not retry the old body.

## Show the owner

The diff again, next to the dry run. Say which rules would be deleted if you sent a partial list. You are sending the full list.

## Failure cases

The owner says yes to a different diff than the one on screen. Stop and merge again.
