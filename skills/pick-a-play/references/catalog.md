# Catalog for the suggestion

## Inputs

query set to the play: engaged, email, or webhook. definition_id when you need one card.

## Call

tables.findable_catalog.list. Free. Use today's price, not a number from an older note.

## Show the owner

The price line you will repeat in the suggestion. If the card says the price varies, say that and do not invent a figure.

## Failure cases

The card is missing. Mark the play unconfirmed and suggest a different one.
