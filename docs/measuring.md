# Measuring

Every number shows its denominator. Unknown is not zero.

Engager tiers: after 30 days, replies and meetings per 100 people by tier, from free sequence reads. Change `tiers.json` only after a learning note and a yes.

Forms: proposed, approved, queued, confirmed by `integrations.health_get`, failed. Queued is not done.

Keyword shares: matched, previewed, approved, posted, failed.

Email stack: holds by reason over time.

Spend: credits per play per week from `workspace.usage.spend_breakdown`.
