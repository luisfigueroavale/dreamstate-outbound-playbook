# Skill frame

Every skill under `skills/` follows this file. The skill stays short. Do not paste this page back into the skill.

## Read

Before a draft, read `brand/AGENTS.md`, then the filled files in `brand.local/`. Status: blank, or a missing file, means stop and ask.

Before a play, read the files that play names under `settings.local/`. If one is missing or still the blank template, stop.

Company facts come from Company Context through `knowledge.context.resolve`. The local files point at those pages. They do not paste them.

## Consent

Nothing sends, enrolls, launches, posts, spends, schedules, pushes to a CRM, or deletes without the owner's yes on that item. Fetch every tool before you run it. The fetched schema wins over this repo.

A paid call says: dry_run first, quote, wait for the owner's yes on that item. Show rows, credits, and quote_hash, then wait. A refused quote is a price, not a yes.

A free read that does not support a dry run can run live. Say that it was a read.

## Facts

Do not invent a metric, a customer, or a product fact. Unknown is not zero. Ids and costs are in `docs/tool-map.md`.

## Writing

Drafts use no em dash and no en dash. Use a period, a comma, or and.

Banned words are the encoded list in `scripts/banned-words.json`. A writing step also passes `docs/ai-tells.md`.

## Ship

The person who drafts does not approve their own send. A ready row is still unsent until a later yes. This repo's v1 does not send.
