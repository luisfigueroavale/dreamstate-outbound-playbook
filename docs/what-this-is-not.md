# What this is not

- Not the decision math for a cold batch. That lives in dreamstate-outbound-engine.
- Not a sender. Nothing in v1 sends, posts, enrolls, or syncs on its own.
- Not a shop for domains or mailboxes. Buying them is outside Dreamstate, and this repo names no seller.
- Not a second copy of the funnel skills that score one fresh post or deliver a file. Those stay in dreamstate-linkedin-funnel.
- Not a price list for plans or seats. Read `workspace.usage.status_get` on your workspace.
